Privacy policy

for the iPhone app Minesweeper

This is a translation. The German version is authoritative.

1. Controller

The controller for data processing in this app is the provider named in the Impressum (legal notice), which also lists our contact details.

2. In short

  • The app has no user account, and we run no servers of our own.
  • Your game state, progress and settings stay on your iPhone. We receive none of it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, but only if you agree. If you decline, the app shows no ads and requests none. You can still play the whole game.

3. Data on your device

The app stores on your iPhone: your progress (level, games in progress, daily puzzles, streak, coins, badges), your settings (vibration, appearance, language) and your answer to the consent form. This data does not leave your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

4. Consent form

At launch, the app asks for your consent to advertising. The form is provided by Google (User Messaging Platform) and follows IAB Europe's Transparency & Consent Framework. So that Google can show the right form and store your answer, the app sends technical data to Google, in particular your IP address and information about your device and the app.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in obtaining your consent lawfully and being able to prove it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Storing your answer on the device is strictly necessary for that (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

You can change or withdraw your decision at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy settings. Withdrawal applies from then on.

5. Advertising with Google AdMob

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). The app shows video ads you can choose to watch, and full-screen ads between games. It shows no banners.

Only with consent. Google's advertising SDK is started only once you have agreed in the consent form. Without consent, the app sends no requests to Google AdMob.

Data processed: IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information such as device model, operating system, language and app version, information about which ads were shown and tapped, and your iPhone's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow it (see section 6).

Purposes: delivering ads, measuring their display and performance, preventing fraud and abuse and, if you have agreed to it, tailoring ads to your interests. The consent form lists each purpose and each advertising partner, and lets you choose them one by one.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for access to your device, § 25(1) TDDDG). Where individual advertising partners rely on a legitimate interest for certain purposes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to that in the consent form.

Transfers to the USA: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

Retention: Google decides how long it keeps the data. See Google's privacy policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency)

If you have agreed in the consent form, iOS additionally asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Google receives your iPhone's advertising identifier only if you allow it. If you decline, you still see ads, but without that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Apple

The app is downloaded from Apple's App Store. Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example about your download and for crash reports if you have agreed to share them with developers. See Apple's privacy policy.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), to object (Art. 21) and to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)). Contact us at the address in the Impressum.

Because we store no data about you ourselves, we usually cannot identify you (Art. 11 GDPR). Requests about advertising data are best sent directly to Google.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.

9. Changes

If the app gains features that process data, we will update this policy.
